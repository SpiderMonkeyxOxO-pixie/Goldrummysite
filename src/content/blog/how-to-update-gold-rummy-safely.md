Because Gold Rummy is installed from a direct APK rather than the Play Store, it doesn't update itself automatically the way most Play Store apps do. That means keeping it current is a manual process — and worth doing carefully, since an update is really just another APK install.

## Why Updates Matter for This Kind of App

Updates typically bring stability fixes, interface changes, and occasionally security patches that address real vulnerabilities. For an app tied to a real-money wallet, skipping updates for a long stretch isn't just about missing new features — it can mean running a version with known issues that a newer release has already fixed.

## Checking Your Current Version

Most apps show their installed version somewhere in a Settings or About screen inside the app itself. Compare that against the current version listed in our [Quick App Information table](/download/) to see whether you're behind.

## How to Update

Updating Gold Rummy uses essentially the same process as the original install:

1. **Download the latest APK** from the [official download page](/download/) — the same source you'd use for a fresh install.
2. **Open the downloaded file.** On most devices, if Gold Rummy is already installed, Android will recognize this as an update rather than a new app and offer an "Update" button instead of "Install."
3. **Confirm the update.** Review any new permissions listed (updates occasionally add new ones) before confirming.
4. **Open the app** and confirm it launches normally and your account and wallet balance are intact.

Because it's recognized as an update rather than a fresh install, your account data, login and app settings are normally preserved — you shouldn't need to log in again or lose any progress, though it's worth confirming this after any major version change.

## If Android Won't Let You Install the Update

If you previously turned off "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager after the original install (which we recommend), you'll need to briefly re-enable it the same way you did originally, then switch it back off once the update finishes. See our [installation guide](/how-to-install/) for the exact steps if you need a refresher.

## A Few Things Worth Checking After Updating

- Confirm your wallet balance matches what you expect before playing a cash table.
- Skim any changelog or update notes the app shows, in case a game rule or fee structure changed.
- If anything looks off with your account after an update, contact Gold Rummy's official in-app support rather than assuming it will resolve itself.

## Keeping Yourself Safe During Updates

The same caution that applies to your original install applies here: download updates only from a source you trust, and be skeptical of any "urgent update required" message that arrives outside the app itself — particularly one that asks you to click a link, enter your password, or share an OTP. Gold Rummy's own app is the only place that should be prompting you for an in-app update.
